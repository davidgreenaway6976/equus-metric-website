Measurement · Nutrition · Conditioning

Four things, done properly.

Not a general-purpose horse app -- four specific problems, solved with real math and real vet-reviewed guidance.

Weight & Body Condition

A girth-and-length weight estimate and a full Henneke 1-9 body condition score, region by region -- with photo history alongside every entry.

Nutrition & Ration Calculator

General guidance is free for every horse -- a personalized daily forage and hard feed split, in kilograms, is the Premium step up.

Care Calendar

Farrier, vaccinations, dental, and the rest, with sensible default frequencies and a real due date -- visible for every horse, free.

Conditioning & Competition Prep

Foundation through taper, matched to your discipline -- with a countdown, a taper recommendation, and a show-week checklist for your next event.
