Privacy Policy

Last updated: draft, not yet published -- this page mirrors the in-app policy word for word and will be updated together with it once it has had a full legal review.

What we collect

Your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase -- we never see or store your password ourselves); the horse profiles, measurements, body condition scores, nutrition and conditioning logs, appointments, and goal events you enter; a photo, if you choose to attach one to a measurement; and basic usage events (e.g. "horse added," "measurement saved") used to understand which features are actually useful.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your horse's registration papers, your address, or any payment card details -- subscription payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google, and we never see your card number.

How it's used

Your horse data powers the app's own features for you -- weight and body condition tracking, ration and conditioning guidance, appointment reminders -- and nothing else. Usage events help us find and fix problems and decide what to build next. Crash reports (via Sentry) help us fix bugs; they may include technical details about your device but not your horse data.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your horse or account data with advertisers. We do not use your data to train third-party AI models.

Who can see it

Only you. Every horse profile, measurement, and record is scoped to your account at the database level -- nobody else's account can read or write it, including other Equus Metric users.

Your data, your control

You can delete your account at any time from Account, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every horse, measurement, and record in it -- immediately, not after a delay -- and cannot be undone.

Third parties we use

Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), RevenueCat (subscription purchase management), and Sentry (crash reporting). Each only receives the data it needs to do its specific job.

Questions

Contact us at support@equusmetric.com with any questions about your data.
